Philosophy · The Inner Map

Six rivers
flowing into one sea

Jungian analytical psychology, Eastern contemplative inquiry, meditation, depth psychotherapy, Five-Element temperament analysis, and breathwork-based somatic practice. Six traditions, each speaking a different language — all flowing toward a single fundamental question.

Six Pillars · The Foundation

Different languages,
one question

Each pillar is an independent tradition. Within The Inner Map, they meet one another deeply — each one opening the other further.

and spirit meet

The East Asian philosophy of the Three Powers — Heaven (spirit), Human (mind), Earth (body) — best describes the integrative structure of The Inner Map. Wholeness arises when all three move together.

🌬 Why breathwork comes first
Deep inner inquiry is not done with the head alone
When the body is tense, even the most careful psychological work circles the surface and goes no further. When breathwork opens the body first, Jungian shadow work and contemplative inquiry descend from the head into the whole body. In that moment, the analysing ego steps briefly aside — and right in that opening, genuine change begins.
How the Pillars Meet

Each tradition deepens
the others

🌬
Breath Regulation
× Inner Inquiry
When the analytical mind settles into the dan-tian through breath regulation, the existential question opens through the whole body rather than the head — a qualitatively different experience.
🜁
Movement Practice
× Shadow Work
Suppressed emotion accumulates in the body. When it is released and metabolised through movement practice, the psychological shadow work that follows is markedly deeper and more lasting.
◎
Still Practice
× Active Imagination
Immediately after the body becomes quiet through still practice, inner images arising in active imagination are far more vivid and alive — and far more useful to the work.
🌙
Five Elements
× Temperament Inquiry
Somatic practice transforms Five-Element temperament from an abstract conceptual framework into a living, felt experience — one you can sense directly in your own body.
