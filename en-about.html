Director · About

A minister, a practitioner, and a walker of many paths

Hong Seung-hwan, Ph.D., has spent a lifetime holding a question that most traditions answer only in part: how do the body, the mind, and the spirit come to live as one?

Trained in Christian counselling psychology, ordained as a minister, and certified as a Kuksundo practitioner — a Korean Taoist somatic tradition — Dr Hong has spent over two decades working with people in the particular vulnerability of special education, where the gap between inner experience and outward expression is most acutely felt.

What became clear over those years is that psychological insight alone is not sufficient. Insight that remains in the head rarely reaches the body — and it is in the body that the deepest patterns are held and the deepest change becomes possible.

The Inner Map was founded as an answer to that realisation: a practice that brings together Jungian depth psychology, Eastern contemplative inquiry, meditation, depth psychotherapy, Five-Element temperament analysis, and breathwork-based somatic practice — each tradition illuminating what the others leave in shadow.

The Journey
A life's worth
of integration

Each training was a door into a dimension the previous one had not fully opened. Together, they became the architecture of The Inner Map.

I
Theology
Ordination & Ministry
Beginning with questions of meaning, transcendence, and the human spirit. Ordained as a minister and working pastorally with individuals and communities navigating major life transitions.
II
Somatic Practice
Kuksundo — Korean Taoist Breathwork
Training in Kuksundo (國仙道), a Korean Taoist tradition integrating dan-tian breathing, movement practice (行功), and still practice (靜功). The discovery that the body is not merely the container of experience — it is the site of transformation.
III
Academic
Ph.D. — Christian Counselling Psychology
Doctoral training in counselling psychology, deepening the theoretical foundation for understanding psychological suffering, relational dynamics, and the conditions under which change becomes possible.
IV
Practice
20+ Years in Special Education
Over two decades working as a special education teacher — a context in which the ordinary assumptions of psychological practice are consistently challenged, and where the connection between body, emotion, and learning is unmistakably clear.
V
Integration
Founding of The Inner Map
The convergence of all previous paths. Jungian depth psychology, Eastern contemplative inquiry, Five-Element temperament analysis, and breathwork-based somatic practice integrated into a single coherent approach to individuation — the journey of becoming, fully, who you already are.
"Body, mind, and spirit are not three separate problems to be solved.
They are one movement — and when they move together, that is wholeness."
Hong Seung-hwan, Ph.D. · The Inner Map
