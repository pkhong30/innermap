Program I · In Detail

10-Session Beginner Coaching

Over five weeks and ten sessions you learn the language of the body, come to understand your own elemental temperament, and knock on the first door of the unconscious. An introductory course for those exploring their inner world for the first time.

Duration
5 Weeks
Focused introductory
journey
Sessions
2× per week
10 sessions total
50 min each
Format
1:1
Online (Zoom)
or In-person
01
Orientation
First Meeting — Reading Your Current Flow
Together we look at your current stress patterns, the structure of tension held in the body, and the rhythms of sleep and relationship. Time to understand, through the body itself, that this is a journey of existential inquiry rather than conventional counselling.
02
Five Elements
Five-Element Temperament Analysis — Understanding Your Natural Current
We map your temperament structure through the energy patterns of Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water. This is not fatalistic — it is a practical inquiry into how energy currently flows and where it has become rigid in your life.
03
Breathing
Dan-tian Breathwork — The Experience of the Body Touching the Present
Stabilising the breath and restoring somatic awareness. When the breath reaches the dan-tian, tension releases and the body returns to the present — a direct, felt experience. Foundational grounding practice for all further inquiry.
04
Body Scan
Mapping the Body — Linking Tension and Emotion
Shoulders, chest, abdomen — we explore where tension has accumulated in the body. Learning to notice the connection between physical sensation and emotion is the first key to psychological inquiry.
05
Persona
Exploring the Persona — What Roles Have You Been Playing?
We examine the gap between the social mask and the authentic self. "The good person," "the capable one," "the caregiver" — we quietly investigate which roles have been exhausting you.
06
Gong Practice
Movement Practice (行功) — Opening the Body Through Motion
Moving beyond still breathwork, we learn foundational gong movements that support the circulation of qi through the body. When the body moves, emotions and energies that have been blocked naturally begin to surface.
07
Shadow · First Encounter
Meeting the Shadow — The Voice of What Has Been Suppressed
Recurring emotional reactions, situations that easily provoke anger, inexplicable fatigue — these are signals from the shadow. We meet them for the first time from a perspective of understanding rather than elimination.
08
Pattern
Recognising Repeating Patterns — What Keeps Coming Back?
We look objectively at recurring patterns in relationship, work, and emotional response. The act of recognising a pattern is already enough to begin changing the flow.
09
Integration
Completing Your Temperament Map — Connecting Body and Psyche
We bring together your Five-Element temperament, the tension patterns of the body, and the first shape of your shadow into a single map. A first answer to the question "who am I?" drawn together with the body.
10
Closing
Integration & Closing — Your First Map
We look back over the five-week journey and design a direction for future inquiry. Body, temperament, and the first shape of the shadow are brought together into a single map as the course draws to a close. Whatever comes next, the flow has already begun.

Ten sessions to understand yourself

Learning the language of the body, mapping your temperament, recognising repeating patterns.
This alone is enough for the flow of your life to begin to shift.

Program II · In Detail

100-Day 1:1 Integrative Coaching
— The Most Complete Journey

100 days is the minimum time a person needs to genuinely change. Over two sessions a week — 50 sessions in total — the body opens, the unconscious surfaces, and life is restructured. The programme moves through stabilisation → exploration → healing → individuation. The aim is not dissolution but the restoration of flow in body and life, and learning to live in harmony with the Self.

1–2
Five-Element Temperament Analysis
Five ElementsMapping the inborn temperament structure through the Five Elements. Cross-referenced with Jung's psychological types. Introduction of breathwork suited to your elemental constitution.
3–4
Breath Regulation — Introductory Practice
BreathingStabilising the breath and restoring somatic awareness. The direct experience of the body returning to the present when breath settles into the dan-tian. Exploring the gap between persona and authentic self.
5–8
Mapping the Flow of Life
Analysing sleep, relationships, daily rhythm, and stress patterns together. Understanding the actual structure of life before engaging in psychological work proper.
9–10
Movement Practice (行功) — Introduction
BreathingFoundational gong movements. The experience of suppressed emotions and blocked energy finding expression through movement as qi circulation opens in the body.
11–12
Persona and Shadow
Exploring social roles and suppressed emotional patterns. Noticing how tension in the body connects to emotional patterns. Preparing for Phase 2.
13–16
Dream Work
Dream WorkDreams are not interpreted as fixed answers. We explore the messages of the unconscious by connecting them to emotion, sensation, and the flow of daily life. First, finding in the body — through dan-tian breathing — the sensations felt in the dream.
17–20
Archetypal Inquiry
Identifying the recurring archetypal patterns at work — Hero, Sage, Inner Child, Anima/Animus. Exploring how each archetype manifests as posture and tension patterns in the body, and how they are projected in relationship.
21–24
Active Imagination
Meditative InquiryEntering into dialogue with the unconscious while the body is open. Active imagination immediately after movement practice. Images from the unconscious arise more clearly when the body has been prepared. Integrating symbolic insight back into connection with lived reality.
25–26
Deepening Dream Work
Analysis of recurring dream patterns and symbols. Connecting the messages of the unconscious to daily life. Confirming how complexes are stored in the body as blocked qi.
27–28
Inner Inquiry
Meditative Inquiry"Who is it that is listening in this moment?" After 30 minutes of still-practice, the inquiry opens in deep stillness. Reviewing the flow of unconscious exploration. Preparing for Phase 3.
29–32
Inner Child Work
Exploring the connection between emotional memories from childhood and tension patterns held in the body. Bringing suppressed emotions into awareness safely. Opening the body's memory of the inner child through spontaneous movement practice.
33–36
Relationship and Boundaries
Understanding recurring relational dynamics, emotional entanglement, and defensive structures. Recognising projection and transference in daily life. Exploring how the body's defensive postures connect to relational patterns. Practising healthy distance and authentic self-expression.
37–38
Forgiveness and Reconciliation
Forgiveness is neither suppression nor idealisation. Receiving the shadow as energy. A process of integration in which psychological energy that has been blocked is able to flow again. The union of opposites is felt in the body as the balancing of qi.
39–40
Reconstructing Life
Redesigning the direction and meaning of your life. Re-reading the Five-Element temperament chart. Beginning to choose a new flow rather than the repetitions of the past. Establishing an individuation plan for the final ten sessions.
41–44
Inquiry into the Self
IndividuationUnderstanding the Self not as a transcendent concept but as the centre that integrates the whole of the psyche harmoniously. Just as the dan-tian is the centre of the body, the Self is the centre of the psyche — experiencing how the body's centre and the psyche's centre are one.
45–46
The Myth of Your Life
Re-reading the story (mythos) that runs through your life. Moving from a narrative centred on wounds to one shaped by meaning and direction. Re-reading the Five-Element temperament in the light of the 100-day journey.
47–48
Embodying Life
Designing concretely how to live within relationship, work, creativity, and practice. Designing the practical methods of an individuated life together.
49–50
Integration Ritual & Closing
Closing RitualClosing movement ritual → integrating body, psyche, and the flow of being into one as the 100-day journey completes. Designing the direction of personal practice going forward. A closing ceremony in which body, mind, and spirit come together.

Safety & Grounding

Grounding and
real-world stability matter deeply to us

While this programme involves deep existential inquiry, real-world grounding and somatic stabilisation are treated as essential. All inquiry takes place on the foundation of grounded, stable daily functioning.

Before the Programme Begins

  • Assessment of current life situation and stress levels
  • Check of bodily tension and breathing patterns
  • Programme suitability orientation
  • Setting personal goals and direction

Programme Principles

  • Integration is always prioritised over dissolution
  • Deep inquiry does not proceed without somatic stabilisation
  • Spiritual experiences are not absolutised
  • Application within real relationships and daily life is treated as essential
