About · The Inner Map

Six streams of wisdom flowing toward one source

The Inner Map is a psychological counselling institute that brings together six distinct traditions — Jungian analytical psychology, Eastern contemplative inquiry, meditation, depth psychotherapy, Five-Element temperament analysis, and breathwork-based somatic practice.

Each tradition speaks a different language. Each, within The Inner Map, speaks to the same fundamental question: how to live as a whole and integrated human being — body, mind, and psyche moving as one.

Why Breathwork Comes First

Deep inquiry cannot happen in a closed body

When the body is tense, even the most careful psychological work stays at the surface. When breathwork opens the body first, Jungian shadow work and inner inquiry descend from the head into the whole body. In that moment the analysing ego steps aside — and genuine change begins.

🌬
Breath Regulation × Inner Inquiry
When the analytical mind settles into the dan-tian, the question opens through the whole body rather than through the head alone.
🜁
Movement Practice × Shadow Work
Suppressed emotion accumulates in the body. When it is released through movement, psychological work deepens.
◎
Still Practice × Active Imagination
Immediately after the body becomes quiet, inner images arise with far greater clarity and vividness.
🌙
Five Elements × Temperament
Somatic practice transforms Five-Element temperament from an abstract concept into a living, felt experience.
